## Technical CAD Portfolio

### 1. 4-Cylinder Crankshaft & Piston Assembly
**Course:** ME-430 Introduction to CAD (NJIT) | **Tool:** SOLIDWORKS

* **Kinematic Assembly:** Modeled a multi-body engine bottom-end assembly including pistons, connecting rods, wrist pins, crankshaft journals, and cylinder bores.
* **Assembly Mates:** Utilized concentric, coincident, and distance mates to define proper mechanical motion and degrees of freedom for the slider-crank linkages.
* **Top View Orthographic Alignment:** Configured top-down visual display states to verify center-line symmetry, rod clearance, and axial positioning along the main crankshaft journals.
* **Visual Presentation:** Applied semi-transparent material appearances to the outer cylinder walls to demonstrate internal piston clearance and linear reciprocating motion.
